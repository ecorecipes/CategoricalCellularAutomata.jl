CategoricalCellularAutomata.jl

Cellular automata over groups as a cartesian category, generalised cellular automata over group homomorphisms, and wiring-diagram composition: the applied-category-theory layer of the ecorecipes cellular-automata ecosystem.

Part of the ecorecipes cellular-automata ecosystem: CellularAutomataCore.jl → CategoricalCellularAutomata.jl (applied category theory) and CellularAutomataCore.jl → EcologicalCellularAutomata.jl (model zoo).

Cellular automata over a group G form a category with finite products, and cellular automata between different groups (generalised cellular automata over group homomorphisms) form a category with weak products over the free product of the groups (Castillo-Ramirez et al., 2025). The constructions extend from sets to alphabets in any category with finite products, together with a categorical Curtis–Hedlund–Lyndon theorem (Castillo-Ramirez et al., 2026). This package implements those constructions on top of CellularAutomataCore.jl, binds them to Catlab's cartesian-category generics, and produces rules that run on any CellularAutomataCore backend.

Status

Implemented: finite groups (cyclic, tori, Cayley tables), configurations, the category of cellular automata over a group with its cartesian structure and Catlab's ThCartesianCategory instance, wiring diagrams evaluated as cellular automata, the Curtis–Hedlund–Lyndon checks, the bridge to and from CellularAutomataCore rules and problems, generalised cellular automata over group homomorphisms with weak products over free products, and linear and monotone automata. Two research extensions go beyond the papers: stochastic automata in a Markov category, and composition of CellularAutomataCore models along undirected wiring diagrams.

Errors

Every exception the package defines subtypes the abstract root CategoricalCellularAutomataError.
